# Primary-source product and content research

Access date:21 October2026 (Asia/Kolkata). These observations guide original implementation. No competitor code, theme, text corpus, sound or artwork was copied. Traffic rank, search volume, retention lift and learning-effect superiority are not established by this research.

Analytics must explain each measure rather than relabeling attempt rate as raw WPM. |

## Shipped word lists and text

Every currently shipped language uses the repository's original CC0 editorial vocabulary compilation in src/lib/content-packs.ts. English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Hindi, Arabic and Latin Gujarati transliteration retain their existing provenance. These are small curated lists, not externally measured frequency lists. The phonetic generator is original; language coverage/quality is described per capability rather than inferred from a language name.

Original passages, code examples and generated-game word pools are documented in [attribution](../../LICENSES/ATTRIBUTION.md). Synthesized sound contains no sampled third-party recording. Self-hosted font license notices stay with the assets.
