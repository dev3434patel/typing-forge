## What changed

Source19 improves mobile replacement/composition feedback, preserves provenance for same-value autocorrect, keeps Arcade deletions silent and highlights the correctly typed target prefix. Zod is pinned to 4.6.7 with the associated compatibility changes. The [exact comparison](../audit/2026-10-21/final19-frozen-source-review-2026-10-27.json) records ten modified and three added paths against source18, with 489 existing paths unchanged.

All 21 brand files, the protected typing-core/backend timing and metric families, and keyboard/caret families retain their source18 hashes. Emitted scene, bloom, Three.js and worker assets retain their bytes; the scene bridge comparison normalizes only one generated bootstrap import filename. Lighthouse acceptance uses the normal first-visit 3D introduction. Isolated typing/game scenarios may disable the opening to measure their own declared scope; that does not establish opening performance.

Two later changes affect acceptance fixtures: [paused-clock liveness diagnosis](../audit/2026-10-21/final19-clock-fixture-liveness-diagnostic.json), [public patch](../audit/2026-10-21/final19-clock-fixture-liveness.patch) and [independent fixture review](../audit/2026-10-21/final19-fixture-delta-review.json). Exactly two E2E files differ across 71 guarded inputs; all 502 product hashes remain unchanged. The fixtures drive the existing preparation fallback while preserving real deadlines and actual readiness/geometry/input assertions. This identifies a fixture contract gap, without proving the cause of every earlier browser failure.
