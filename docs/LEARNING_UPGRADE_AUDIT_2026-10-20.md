## 2. What was implemented

The new `/lessons` route teaches **English / US QWERTY** through **36 lessons, six chapters and 108 original passages**. Thirty foundation lessons progress sequentially. Six speed lessons form a separate sequence, starting immediately at 15 WPM so experienced users can train speed without completing the foundation course first.

| Chapter | Lessons | Main requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home row | 6 | Complete the assigned text correctly; at least 95% original input accuracy. |
| Upper row | 6 | Introduce upper-row reaches and connect them to the home row; at least 95% input accuracy. |
| Full alphabet | 6 | Introduce the lower row and combine the alphabet; at least 97% input accuracy. |
| Capitals and numbers | 6 | Practise Shift, capitalization and digit reach; at least 97% input accuracy. |
| Punctuation | 6 | Practise pauses, apostrophes, questions, structure and paired punctuation; at least 98% input accuracy. |
| Speed ladder | 6 | 15 / 25 / 35 / 45 / 60 / 80 WPM, at least 98% input accuracy, a full uninterrupted 30 seconds. |

Each lesson needs **two qualifying, different passage variants**. A repeat of an already qualifying variant reinforces practice without inventing another checkpoint. Finite lessons require the complete correct passage, at least 40 insertion attempts and three active seconds. Speed lessons require at least 25 insertion attempts, the stated WPM, clean processed text and natural timeout after 30 active seconds. Idle time counts; pausing or manually finishing cannot qualify for a speed checkpoint. Corrected mistakes remain in the input-accuracy denominator.

The course uses the existing canonical controller, current-word correction policy, first-input clock and result repository. The shared JSON catalog at [typing-course-v1.json](../backend/resources/typing-course-v1.json) supplies the browser and PHP. Timed targets repeat their original variant deterministically to at least 2,400 ASCII characters, avoiding target-extension ambiguity.

## 4. Persistence, privacy and trust limits

Course progress is a **personal milestone**, not proof of human input, a public rank or a certificate. The model prefers a server's `accepted.course` projection when present, including an unqualified result; it never replaces a failed server projection with a passing local summary. Unaccepted on-device summaries remain intentionally **local-unverified** and are not tamper-proof against a person controlling their own browser storage.

Raw replay normally expires after 90 days. The compact course projection can persist until session/account deletion, retaining lesson/variant identity, measured totals, checks and completion time. Existing public-corpus per-key learning projections can also retain expected/typed characters and timing after raw replay expiry. That is distinct from deleting raw replay and is disclosed in the product's privacy/data controls. This report does not claim that every trace of typed practice disappears after 90 days.

The current application uses typed PHP API calls, secure cookie sessions, CSRF and MySQL/MariaDB persistence. Historical Supabase SQL/functions remain reference material rather than the active account backend. The native data lifecycle passed **15 groups**, and race/ranked lifecycle passed **10 groups**, on the isolated local configuration; see [common lifecycle process](../audit/learning-upgrade-2026-10-20/php-common-lifecycle-process.json), [data receipt](../audit/learning-upgrade-2026-10-20/php-data-lifecycle-common-final.json) and [race receipt](../audit/learning-upgrade-2026-10-20/php-race-lifecycle-common-final.json). The final canonical PHP differential run records **393 cases, zero mismatches**: 377 valid references and 16 rejection cases, including Latin/NFC, Arabic, Devanagari, Gujarati, Bengali and emoji. See the [fresh parity receipt](../audit/learning-upgrade-2026-10-20/php-replay-parity-final.log). The new course parity adds its separately reported 136-case scope; these totals are not an aggregate test count.

## 7. Remaining limits and release work

- This formal course covers English / US QWERTY. Existing alternate layouts/languages remain available in adaptive/general practice; they do not become equivalent finger-placement courses. No native device or actual finger detection is claimed.
- Physical phones/tablets, native Safari/iOS, actual Android soft keyboards, operating-system IMEs/dead keys, NVDA/VoiceOver and manual accessibility review remain **UNVERIFIED**. Automated tests and contrast samples cannot establish full WCAG conformance.
- Thresholds and two-variant progression are explicit product rules. Learning effectiveness, retention, classroom administration and the breadth of a full school curriculum require real-user/editorial evaluation.
- This upgrade preserves the full intro source and confirms the normal full-quality worker in three engines. WebKit worker capability, every historical route/combination and a complete all-browser product rerun remain outside that finite confirmation.
- Public hosting, HTTPS/cookie/CORS configuration, database backup/recovery, production contention and deployment-specific CSP/cache behavior remain **UNVERIFIED**. The local Apache/PHP/MySQL harness does not certify an arbitrary host.
- The updated CI workflow includes the native course gates. Remote GitHub Actions execution is not certified by these local receipts.
- The build intentionally omits a sitemap until `SPAROWTYPE_SITE_URL` is configured. Production canonical URLs, sitemap discoverability and search indexing need host configuration and verification. New route/title/navigation source is implemented.
- Email/password authentication is verified locally. OAuth and delivered verification/password-reset email are unavailable in the current PHP setup; historical Supabase accounts have not been migrated automatically.
- Field INP, production load and native-device experience remain UNVERIFIED. The completed selected-flow and label confirmations have their finite scopes in section 5. No tests establish universal perfection or prove that a browser transcript came from a human.
