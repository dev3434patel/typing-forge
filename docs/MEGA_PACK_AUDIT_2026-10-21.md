### Retention and award qualification

Raw replay and reward aggregates are different records. A personal reward receipt holds source identity/hash, counts, versioned award facts and aggregated key/finger/ngram sufficient statistics; it does not keep a second complete ordered passage log. **These PERSONAL aggregates remain after one source session is deleted, until the separate workspace/progression clear or acknowledged account deletion removes them.** Deleting a session prevents new source claims and removes its raw evidence, not a historical append-only award ledger. Ordinary personal course/placement projections follow their session/owner record's deletion contract. Public-corpus learning projections can retain ordered key/timing evidence under the existing disclosed learning contract; raw expiry is not a promise to erase every character-level projection.

Local and server-verified trust scopes remain separate. Current arcade/Daily/BOT practice is not converted into a verified online ranking. A classroom share checks canonical mathematics, not a student's identity or independent physical typing. Aggregated receipts retain deterministic award arithmetic after raw expiry; indefinite recomputation from the original event stream is deliberately not claimed.
